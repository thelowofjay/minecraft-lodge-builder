Methodology, counting rules, and the mildly irritating parts
  • Scope: 225 Mini-category memberships plus 104 Micro-category memberships as displayed on 2026-10-30.
  • Deduplication: 15 products appeared in both categories. Matching GetFPV SKUs were merged, leaving 314 unique products.
  • Counting: each product listing counts once. KV/color choices inside one listing are not separate motors unless GetFPV created separate product listings.
  • Square / rectangular: explicit adjacent-axis notation such as 9×9, 12×12, 16×16, or 16×19 mm.
  • Diameter / PCD: explicit Ø, Φ, D, or PCD-style notation. Screw-hole diameter is excluded when GetFPV also supplies a square pattern.
  • Ambiguous / other: bare values such as “12 mm,” thread-only descriptions, three-hole/triangular wording, or family shorthand that does not define the measurement relationship.
  • Missing: GetFPV supplies no usable motor-mount spacing. Manufacturer verification was reserved for the 13 ambiguous listings; omissions were not silently replaced in the GetFPV counts.
  • Measurement totals: a motor supporting two patterns contributes once to each measurement, so measurement totals can exceed the number of classified products.
